> Ten episodes on a fogged-in New England island that everyone except the mayor believes is cursed — and he is busy selling it as the next Nantucket. **Matthew Rhys is the best thing here by a mile**: his Tom Loftis meets every impossible thing on that island with a straight face and a to-do list, and it is **an absolute delight** to watch. The horror and the comedy run on the same beat instead of taking turns. Not every thread on the island lands, but Rhys never misses.
