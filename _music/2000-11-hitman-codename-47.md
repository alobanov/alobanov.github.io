> Jesper Kyd's first Hitman score — nine tracks of pure electronics, written inside the memory limits of DirectMusic. **Another game out of my childhood**: every time I hear this, warm memories come back of playing it the year it came out. The score never got a proper release of its own — it only reached streaming officially for the 25th anniversary of Hitman.
