> A group of teenagers on their last night together, replaying formative memories to a period playlist — from Beethoven & Dinosaur, the studio behind The Artful Escape. **More an interactive experience than an actual game**. **The nostalgia lands even if you did not grow up in the USA** — the vibe comes straight out of the teen movies we all watched, and the problems you have at that age are the same wherever you live. A solid 3, but **very much an acquired taste**: it will not click with everyone.
