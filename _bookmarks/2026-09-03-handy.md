### 🎙️ [Handy](https://handy.computer/) <small class="superscript">Dictation</small>

Hold a hotkey, speak, release — it types what you said into whatever field is focused. Whisper or Parakeet run on your own machine, so the audio never leaves it. Free, open source, Mac, Windows and Linux.
