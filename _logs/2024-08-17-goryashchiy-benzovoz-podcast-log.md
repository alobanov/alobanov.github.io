> A weekly stream of news, opinions and forecasts about popular culture — what
> to watch, what to play, and what just happened in the industry. Hosted by
> Ivan Talachev and Vadim Elistratov, former editor-in-chief of DTF and
> Kinopoisk, running since 2022. Episodes go two and a half to four hours, and
> the jokes carry as much of it as the news does.
